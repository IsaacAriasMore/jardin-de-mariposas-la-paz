Cookies and Similar Technologies Policy

Transparency about the technical preferences the website currently stores.

This policy describes technologies observable in the current website. A cookie banner has not been added because no non-essential tracking technologies have been identified in the website code.

Current local storage

The site uses browser local storage, not cookies, to remember two visitor-selected preferences:

  • jmlp-theme-v1: light or dark mode preference;
  • jmlp-motion-preference-v2: full or reduced motion preference.

Purpose and control

These preferences help preserve the selected appearance and motion experience when the site reloads. You can change both preferences through the visible controls on the site or clear local storage in your browser settings.

External services and infrastructure

The site may include external links or services such as WhatsApp, Facebook and Google Maps. When using them, their providers may apply their own technologies and policies.

Hosting infrastructure may use technically necessary mechanisms. Its actual operation must be audited during staging before publication on Hostinger; this policy does not state that no other infrastructure technologies exist.

Future technologies

If we add analytics, advertising, forms, newsletters or other similar technologies in the future, this policy will be updated before or when those tools are activated, as appropriate.
