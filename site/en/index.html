Guided experience

Butterfly Garden Tour

During the visit, you will discover how butterflies live and develop. Learn about their life cycle, from egg and caterpillar to chrysalis and adult butterfly, as well as their behaviour, feeding and reproduction.

Your guide will also explain how environmental conditions influence their development, characteristics that help us understand them better, and their role within the ecosystem.

ICT-certified guideService in Spanish and English

An experience for many ages, from kindergarten and school groups to families, adults and older adults.
