The butterfly garden

Our Butterfly Garden

Explore a place where you can observe butterflies surrounded by vegetation and flowers.

Jardín de Mariposas La Paz is located in Bajo La Paz, San Ramón, Alajuela, surrounded by the natural setting of this part of Costa Rica.

The butterfly garden offers a place to observe butterflies up close and learn more about their life cycle on a guided walk among vegetation and flowers.

A guided experience

Explore the butterfly garden with a guide

The visit is led by a guide and is designed for many ages, from kindergarten and school groups to families, adults and older adults.

The guide is ICT-certified and provides service in Spanish and English. During the tour, you can learn about butterfly life cycles, habits, feeding and their role within the ecosystem.
