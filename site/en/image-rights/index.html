Legal information

Photography and Image Rights

How we distinguish rights in a photograph from the image rights of people shown in photographs.

This page explains current practices and the channels for requesting a review. It does not replace the licenses, authorizations or permissions that apply to each specific material.

Photographs and third-party materials

Some photographs have been taken by Jardín de Mariposas La Paz staff to document the space and visits. Other images belong to third parties and are used under applicable licenses or authorizations.

Rights in a photograph and the image rights of a person shown in it are different concepts. Third-party rights remain with their owners.

People photographed and minors

Jardín de Mariposas La Paz seeks authorization before using visitor photographs on the website or social media.

When an identifiable minor is included in promotional material, we will seek authorization from a parent, mother or responsible representative before publication. Doubtful cases require internal review before launch.

Photos taken by visitors

Visitors may take photographs and record video during their visit for personal purposes and normally share them on social media. This policy does not seek to claim rights in photographs a visitor took.

Commercial use of official materials

Use of the name, visual identity, official materials or content belonging to Jardín de Mariposas La Paz for commercial or promotional purposes, including use to promote other butterfly gardens, butterfly sales, chrysalis sales or related activities, requires prior authorization.

You may request it through the official email or WhatsApp.

Requests for review

If you believe you appear in a photograph, that material you authored is being used, or that there is a rights issue, you may write to the official email or contact the published telephone numbers to request a review. Each request will be reviewed; we do not promise automatic removal without that review.
