Butterflies

Butterflies

Discover the fascinating world of butterflies: their transformation, habits and relationship with their surroundings.

Life cycle

A remarkable transformation

A butterfly’s life cycle moves through several stages: egg, caterpillar, chrysalis and adult butterfly. During the tour, you can understand this process better and discover how it changes throughout development.

Their behaviour, feeding and development depend on plants and the conditions of the environment where they live.

A closer look

During the visit, pausing to observe a butterfly lets you appreciate its wings and the place where it rests.

A diversity of tones

A look at the variety of colours and forms found in the world of butterflies.

Observe

You do not need to know butterflies to enjoy them

You do not need to know butterflies to enjoy them. During the tour, you can observe them up close and learn as you walk through the butterfly garden.
