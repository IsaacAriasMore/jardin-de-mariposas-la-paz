Learning during the visit

The butterfly life cycle

The tour lets you learn about different aspects of butterfly life: behaviour, feeding, reproduction and the stages of transformation.

During the visit, you can also learn about the conditions they need to develop, their relationship with plants and the environment, and their importance within ecosystems.
