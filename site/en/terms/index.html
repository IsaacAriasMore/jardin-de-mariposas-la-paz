Basic terms for using this informational website and asking about a visit.

Identity and purpose of the site

Jardín de Mariposas La Paz provides this website mainly for informational and promotional purposes about the butterfly garden, the visitor experience, hours, location and contact channels.

The information is intended to help plan a visit, but may require direct confirmation before travelling.

Enquiries, visits and availability

Enquiries are made on WhatsApp. An initial conversation does not guarantee availability; a visit is considered arranged when the people responsible confirm the details directly.

Current hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.; Saturday, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon; Sunday, check availability. We recommend confirming availability before travelling.

Prices, payment and changes

Price information is confirmed directly when you ask. Unless something different is agreed directly, payment for the visit is made at the butterfly garden.

There is no published formal cancellation policy. If you need to change or cancel an arranged visit, we recommend contacting us on WhatsApp as soon as possible.
